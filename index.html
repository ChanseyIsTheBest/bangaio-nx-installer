bangaio-nx installer

Builds the Nintendo Switch package of Bangai-O HD: Missile Fury (Xbox Live Arcade) from your own copy. Everything runs in this browser: nothing is uploaded.

1. Choose your game

Your Xbox 360 package of the game: title 58410AF2, the file in Content/0000000000000000/58410AF2/000D0000/ of a console's storage. Or the folder the game was extracted to (default.xex, media/, ...). The game has no title update.

Nothing chosen yet.

2. Create the package

The page makes the game's shaders from your files and compiles them for the Switch, which takes a few minutes and a few GB of memory. The zip then downloads while it is written (about 300 MB).

3. Copy it to the Switch

  1. Extract bangaio-nx.zip to the root of the SD card: it makes switch/bangaio-nx/.
  2. Start bangaio-nx.nro from the Homebrew Menu with a title takeover (hold R while starting a game).
  3. Its settings are in switch/bangaio-nx/renut.toml: docked_resolution (900 or 1080), smaa (true or false) and language ("english", "japanese" or "french"). Making the package again gives a fresh renut.toml: keep yours if you changed it.
